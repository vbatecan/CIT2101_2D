## 1. Narrative Synopsis

At 7:45 PM, shortly after the "Bean & Binary" artisan cafe closes its doors to the public, startup founder Kurt Miguel Ancheta calls the emergency dispatch in sheer distress. 

His company, *AetherCore Systems*, was hours away from pitching a revolutionary quantum encryption engine to venture capitalists. The only copy of the proprietary source code and physical biometric hardware key was housed on a ruggedized SSD drive—the "Aether Drive"—kept in Kurt's locked briefcase in the cafe's mezzanine office. The drive is gone.

Kurt had spent the late afternoon in an intense strategy session with his lead developer and minority partner, Shanaia Ortega. Shanaia claims the meeting concluded amicably at 5:30 PM, after which she took a rideshare straight to her apartment across town, turned off her work phone, and never returned to the premises.

However, forensic digital extraction from Kurt's mobile phone and private back-alley surveillance stills reveal a calculated conspiracy of industrial espionage and preemptive retaliation.

## 7. Epilogue & Narrative Resolution

* **Victory Condition (`CasesWIN.png`):** Detective Borja recovers the encrypted prototype drive from Shanaia's vehicle console. The intellectual property is returned to Kurt, and Shanaia is taken into custody for trade secret theft and burglary. Kurt offers Shan a long-term commercial catering contract in gratitude.
* **Failure Condition (`Case3FAILED.png`):** The 300-second case timer expires. Shanaia's high-priced corporate defense attorney arrives, claims the blurry camera still is inadmissible, and removes Shanaia from the premises. By morning, the proprietary code is uploaded to an overseas mirror, destroying AetherCore Systems.
